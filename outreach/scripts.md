# Outreach Script Pack — WhatsApp Appointment System (Rs. 5,000)

Copy-paste ready. Replace `[Clinic Name]` before sending. Add one personalization line per message — it takes 60 seconds and doubles reply rates.

**Golden rule:** 10 real conversations tonight. Not 100 spam messages.

---

## Message 1 — First contact (send today)

> Assalamualaikum. I'm a software engineering student based in Abbottabad and I build simple appointment/WhatsApp systems for local clinics.
>
> I made a small demo showing how a patient can choose a service, request an appointment and send the details directly to WhatsApp instead of just calling.
>
> I can customize a similar system for [Clinic Name] for Rs. 5,000.
>
> If you're interested, I can send you the demo link/video so you can see it first. No commitment.

**Personalization ideas (pick one):**
- "I noticed your clinic on [Google Maps / Facebook] — I couldn't find an online appointment option, which is why I'm reaching out."
- "I walked past your clinic on [area] and thought of this."
- "A friend of mine visits [Clinic Name] and mentioned it."

**Never mention:** AI, Python, FastAPI, agents, tech stack. They only care: *"Will this get me more patients and fewer lost calls?"*

---

## Message 2 — They said "send demo"

> Sure. Here is the demo.
>
> The idea is simple: a patient opens the clinic page, checks the services, enters their preferred appointment time, and the system prepares a WhatsApp message with all the patient details.
>
> I can customize the clinic name, services, branding and WhatsApp number for [Clinic Name].
>
> If you like the idea, I can make the customized version for Rs. 5,000.

**Attach:** the demo link (host it — see README) or the 3 screenshots.

**Best format for WhatsApp:** a 30–60 second screen recording walking through:
1. Tap a service → 2. Fill the form → 3. WhatsApp opens with the full message → 4. Dashboard with the lead.

## Message 4 — "Too expensive"

Do NOT drop to Rs. 1,000. Say:

> I understand.
>
> Since I'm building my first few local client projects, I can offer the complete basic version for Rs. 4,000 for the first few clinics.
>
> That includes the appointment page, WhatsApp integration and lead management.
>
> If you'd like, I can first show you exactly how it works before you decide.

That Rs. 4,000 is the **first-client price**, not your permanent price.

## Objection cheat sheet

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already have reception / phone" | "This catches patients after hours and when the line is busy — those are the ones you lose today." |
| "No one books online here" | "They don't need an account or app — one tap and it's in your WhatsApp, like any patient message." |
| "What if it stops working?" | "It's a simple page, not an app — nothing to crash. If anything needs changing, I'm local and reachable." |
| "Send me a proposal" | "I'll send 3 screenshots and a 60-second video — easier than a proposal. If it makes sense, we'll talk." |
| "Can you also make a Facebook page / Google listing?" | Yes — but keep the main offer fixed; upsell separately (Rs. 2,000–3,000 each, later). |
